Our Story

From missed calls to intelligent conversations.

Founded in 2025, Vani was built to replace hold music and missed calls with AI agents that sound human. We started in India, where millions of businesses lose customers to unanswered phones every day. Today, we serve businesses globally — from solo founders to enterprises handling millions of calls a month.

Our founding team comes from voice infrastructure, AI research, and enterprise SaaS. We obsess over latency, voice quality, and the seamless handoff between human and machine. Because when your customers call, every millisecond matters.
