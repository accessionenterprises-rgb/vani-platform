Questions

You're billed based on voice minutes used. Starter and Pro plans include a bank of minutes each month. Any usage beyond that is charged at your plan's per-minute rate. No hidden fees, no platform charges.

Overage minutes are billed at your plan's per-minute rate. On Starter, that's $0.10/min. On Pro, $0.08/min. You'll see real-time usage in your dashboard so there are no surprises.

Free minutes cover everything: STT, LLM processing, and TTS. There are no separate charges for AI inference. One minute of voice = one minute billed.

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your dashboard. When you upgrade, the new rate applies immediately. Unused minutes don't roll over between billing cycles.

Phone numbers are provisioned through Twilio at their standard rates. You can bring your own Twilio account or use ours. Number costs are separate from voice minutes.

Enterprise adds dedicated infrastructure (your own servers, not shared), a custom SLA with uptime guarantees, SOC2 compliance documentation, custom integrations with your existing systems, and a dedicated account manager. Volume pricing is negotiated based on your usage.
